This guide is a starting point for a conversation about material selection. The right panel depends on the intended application, moisture exposure, structural requirements, installation details and the product's verified technical specification.

Start with the application

Write down where the panel will be used, what it will support, how it will be finished and whether it may be exposed to moisture. Share these details with your architect, fabricator or material supplier.

Compare the specification

Ask for the grade, thickness, applicable standard, test documents, warranty terms and current availability. Do not choose based on a product name alone.

Ask before ordering

  • Is this grade suitable for the intended use?
  • Which thickness does the design or fabricator specify?
  • What documentation is available for the product?
  • What are the quantity, delivery and warranty terms?
